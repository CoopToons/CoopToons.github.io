Privacy Policy for Tile & Tell
Effective date: 12-Nov-2026

CoopToons develops Tile & Tell. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

For privacy questions or requests, contact cooptoons@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device
Tile & Tell stores puzzle progress, completion information, and preferences, such as appearance and sound settings, locally on your device.

The app does not require you to create a CoopToons account. CoopToons does not operate a server or cloud-storage service for your puzzle progress.

Advertising
Tile & Tell uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information through the advertising services, including:

IP address and approximate location.

Device or app identifiers.

Information about advertisements shown and interactions with advertisements.

App interactions, diagnostic information, and performance information.

This information may be used to deliver and measure advertising, provide analytics relating to advertising, and maintain or improve advertising services. The information processed depends on the SDK configuration, your privacy choices, and applicable requirements. Google describes these data practices in its Mobile Ads SDK disclosures.

Google’s handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy, available through Google’s Privacy & Terms website.

Advertising privacy choices
Where required, the app presents privacy messages so you can make choices about advertising-related data use.

When available, you can revisit those choices through “Advertising Privacy Choices” in the app’s Settings.

If the app requests permission to track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites, you can allow or decline that request through Apple’s permission prompt and manage that permission in your device settings.

Game Center
Tile & Tell uses Apple Game Center for features such as leaderboards and achievements. When those features are used, the app may submit completion scores and achievement progress to Apple.

Apple processes Game Center information under its own privacy terms. Game Center information may include your nickname, identifiers, scores, achievements, and gameplay activity. Visibility to other players depends on the feature and your Game Center privacy settings.

You can manage Game Center preferences through Apple’s settings. For further information, see Apple’s “Game Center & Privacy” notice.

Other analytics and accounts
CoopToons does not use Firebase, a separate analytics service, a separate crash-reporting service, or a CoopToons user-account system in Tile & Tell.

This does not exclude diagnostic, performance, or advertising measurement performed by Google’s advertising SDK, or information processed by Apple through Game Center.

Contacting us
If you email CoopToons, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message. We use that information to respond to your inquiry and handle related support or privacy requests.

Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

Information sharing
The app uses Google’s advertising services and Apple Game Center as described above. Those providers process information under their respective policies.

CoopToons may also disclose information received through support communications when necessary to comply with legal obligations or protect legal rights.

Retention and deletion
Locally stored app information remains on your device until it is removed or otherwise deleted. Removing local app data does not necessarily remove information held separately by Apple or Google.

Information processed by Apple and Google is subject to their retention policies and applicable privacy controls.

CoopToons retains support communications as needed to respond to inquiries, resolve issues, and meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of information you have provided directly to CoopToons, subject to applicable exceptions.

Children’s privacy
Tile & Tell is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children.

CoopToons does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to CoopToons, contact cooptoons@gmail.com so we can review and address the situation.

Your privacy rights
Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including access, correction, deletion, or choices about certain uses and disclosures.

For information held directly by CoopToons, contact cooptoons@gmail.com. For information controlled by Apple or Google, use their privacy controls or contact those providers.

Changes to this policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes to the app, its services, or applicable requirements. The effective date at the top will identify the latest published version.
